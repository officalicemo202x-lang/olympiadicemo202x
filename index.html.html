Curriculum Breakdown

Complete 11th & 12th Mathematics Syllabus

"This exam isn't made to complete it, it is made to observe how a student can penetrate."

— I.CEMO Academic Council Philosophy
CLASS 11TH

Class 11th Syllabus

• Sets & Relations: Types of sets, Venn diagrams, Cartesian product, Domain & Range.

• Trigonometric Functions: Trigonometric identities, Compound angles, General solutions of equations.

• Complex Numbers & Quadratic: Argand plane, Discriminant analysis, Roots of unity.

• Permutations & Combinations: Counting principles, Factorials, nPr & nCr applications.

• Sequences & Series: AP, GP, Sum to n terms, AGP series.

• Straight Lines: Slopes, Angles, Distance formula, Family of lines.

• Conic Sections: Circles, Parabolas, Ellipses, Hyperbolas standard equations.

• Limits & Derivatives: First principles, Standard limits, Product & Quotient rules.

• Statistics & Probability: Mean deviation, Variance, Axiomatic probability.

CLASS 12TH

Class 12th Syllabus

• Matrices & Determinants: Operations, Inverse, System of linear equations, Properties.

• Inverse Trigonometry: Domain, Range, Principal value branches, Algebraic properties.

• Continuity & Differentiability: Implicit differentiation, Logarithmic & Parametric differentiation.

• Applications of Derivatives: Rate of change, Monotonicity, Tangents & Normals, Maxima & Minima.

• Integral Calculus: Definite & Indefinite integrals, Partial fractions, Integration by parts.

• Differential Equations: Order, Degree, Homogeneous & First-order linear DE.

• Vectors & 3D: Vector products, Shortest distance between lines, Equations of planes.

• Probability & LPP: Conditional probability, Bayes' Theorem, Linear Programming.
